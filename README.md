# **Little Women (2019)**

It is a coming-of-age film directed by Greta Gerwig, based on Louisa May Alcott's novel of the same name. It is about the story of the four March sisters—Meg, Jo, Beth, and Amy.

**Notable Quote:**
>Women, they have minds, and they have souls, as well as just hearts. And they’ve got ambition, and they’ve got talent, as well as just beauty.
